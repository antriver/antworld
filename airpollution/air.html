Aerosols   Back

Most aerosol manufacturers have agreed  to phase out the use of CFC gasses. These damage the 

ozone layer and are causing global warming. Many of the main supermarket chains are going to stop

selling products with CFC gasses in them.

 

 

 

 

Nuclear Waste   Back

Nuclear power stations produce radioactive wastes which are very hard to dispose

of safely. Every radioactive substance has a half-life, which is how long it will last for. 

Nuclear reactors produce plutonium 239 which has a half-life of 240,000 years and it

will still be dangerous for half a million years.

 

 

 

 

 

  Lead Pollution  Back

Most of the lead that pollutes the atmosphere comes from the exhaust of vehicles that

run on lead petrol. A car ca be converted to run on lead free petrol for just £50

 

 

 

 

Cigarettes   Back

People who smoke pollute the air around them and have the risk of getting an illness and

developing cancer. The substances which people breathe in when they smoke are carbon particles,

carbon monoxide, tar and nicotine all of which can cause breathing problems.

 

 

 

 

  Fossil Fuels   Back

Factories and power stations burn fossil fuels to generate energy. Burning fossil

fuels releases smoke which contains gasses such as carbon dioxide and nitrogen oxide.

Power stations burning fossil fuels have tall chimneys which release the smoke high up

into the atmosphere.
